## What it is
A preindustrial world simulator: a living, data-backed Earth. Electron app (`app/`) plus a Rust
no_std engine compiled to wasm (`engine/`). Climate, ice ages, sea level, rivers, plants, fire,
herds, pests and about 50 kinds of natural disaster are simulated tile by tile. Since v0.32,
Stone Age peoples (hunter-gatherer tribes) live on the map (engine/src/tribes.rs). The long-term aim is
to carry people from the Paleolithic to the modern day and beyond (see "People: the plan").

## How the owner likes to work
- Plain English, short sentences, active voice, no emojis, no filler. Say what was done, not how hard it was.
- Work autonomously when they are away; make sensible calls and say which reading you took.
- Be honest about limits and measured numbers. They asked "are you sure it is high quality?"
  and a fresh review found real bugs. Review your own work with a separate agent before claiming done.
- Deliver three zips each release (from v0.30 named Odyssey-vX.Y.Z-...): `Odyssey-vX.Y.Z-setup.zip` (app + data, installs with Setup.cmd on
  Windows), `-setup-part2-16km.zip` (the n480 files; one zip breaks the 30 MB upload limit) and `-source.zip`.

## Build and test
- Engine: `cd engine && RUSTC_BOOTSTRAP=1 cargo build --offline --release --target wasm32-unknown-unknown`,
  then `sh tools/build-engine.sh` (copies to `app/data/engine.wasm`). Profiling: `--features prof` with
  `RUSTFLAGS="-C link-arg=--allow-undefined"` (the host supplies env.prof_now; PROF has 24 slots).
  v0.34 was built with rustc 1.97.0 (sin/cos come from libm, see lib.rs libm_shim); the world without people
  is byte-identical to v0.33's (tools/fingerprint.js).
- No wasm target and static.rust-lang.org blocked: fetch the library source from GitHub
  (`git clone --depth 1 --branch 1.95.0 --filter=blob:none --sparse https://github.com/rust-lang/rust.git rustsrc`,
  then `git -C rustsrc sparse-checkout set library`) and run `sh tools/setup-wasm-toolchain.sh rustsrc/library`.
- Engine changes meant to be exact (speed-ups): `node tools/fingerprint.js <old.wasm> <new.wasm> 160 1200 0 259 11`
  (and mode 1, and pstart 258) must print equal hashes. For save/load changes: `node tools/load_check.js`.
- Where the time goes: `node --cpu-prof` on a run gives per-function times for the wasm (inline(never) on a
  helper splits it out of `step`); `tools/stalls.js` lists the slowest months.
- The data files (`app/data/*.bin`) are not in the source zip; take them from the setup zip
  (it holds the 16 km files too). `earth-n28.bin` (title globe) must ship in the setup zip.
- People: `node tools/tribe_check.js [years] [seed]` (15 checks), `tools/band_probe.js [years] [seed] [n] [every]`
  (bands, techs, kits, adaptation by year), `DISPLAY=:99 python3 tools/ui_people.py <electron> app <shots>`.
- Quick checks with Node (`tools/world_node.js`): run 120 months at n160 for speed (~44 ms a month;
  16 km ~390 ms), a save/load round trip, event counts over 20-40 years, fire totals
  (~1.2-1.5 Mkm2 burned a year).
- UI: `DISPLAY=:99 python3 tools/ui_test.py <electron> app <shots> [160|480]` with a Linux Electron 33.2.1
  (github.com/electron/electron releases) under `Xvfb :99` (Playwright is preinstalled). Software GL gives

- Packaging: copy `app/` without node_modules and without `*-n320/n80.bin` (keep n28, n160, n480 and

## Things that bite
- The engine runs 1-3 months ahead of the clock. Anything shown on the map or in the log must be
  matched to the clock's month (`histAt`, `queueLog`), not the engine's newest.
- Track points are 8 floats (`TRK_W`): id, month, lat, lon, wind, basin (100 + EF = tornado), day, gale radius.
  Cyclone ids restart after a load: key by month and id.
- `ow_fire_pack` is 6 bytes a tile: fire, scar, fog, flood water, mark, mark kind. It has its own buffer.
- Save format v10 plus "extra" blocks (more.rs); block 15 holds flood water and marks. Old saves must keep loading.
- Food: `derive_tile` sets the long-run crop and pasture food (`mo.food_c`, `mo.food_h`) at climate sweeps;
  `food_live` (yearly) scales them by salt, silt and grass into `food[i]`. Don't put erosion in both.
- Animals are shares (0-1) of what the land feeds; a fall is capped per year and they return from neighbours.
- The rarity ladder (1-in-10 to 1-in-100,000 years per ~600 km region) in `events.js` comes from
  1,200 simulated years; rerun `long_run.js` + `rarity.js` after retuning a hazard.
- `rng` draws change the weather sequence: adding one shifts every later event, so compare event
  counts over several seeds, not one.
- WebAssembly returns addresses as signed i32: past 2 GB of engine memory (the 16 km map) they come out
  negative. `sim.js` and `tools/world_node.js` wrap every export that returns an address (`_ptr`, and a
  named list) with `>>> 0`; add new ones there. Take the address before reading `x.memory.buffer`: a call
  that grows memory detaches the old buffer.
- The host frees its input buffers after `ow_init` and a save's bytes after `ow_load` (`ow_free`); the
  engine must never keep a pointer into them.
- A load steps back one month and replays it. Everything the month adds to a yearly sum must come back
  as it stood when that month began: save extras 17 (year totals, `fl_n`, last year end's events),
  18 (each river reach's flood record, by tile) and 19 (lake sums) do that. A replayed December skips its
  year end (already in the save) except the rivers' (`replay_dec`); reaches with no saved record are not
  judged at the next year end (`riv_norec`). A new yearly sum needs the same treatment.
- The river network a load rebuilds can differ slightly from the saved one (a few reaches more or fewer in
  random worlds): anything per river slot must be saved by tile, not by slot number.

## People: what is built (v0.34, with v0.35 changes in README-SOURCE)
- engine/src/tribes.rs. No fixed ages: the redesign doc says change must come from surplus, storage, density and
  sedentism (the six dials, not built yet). Nothing in the engine asks "is this human": each people has a species.
- Species cards (`Species`, `sapiens()`): mass (food need ~ mass^0.75), build (cold), brain, birth gap and first
  birth (Kaplan et al. 2000: 41 months, 19.7 years), invention, skill memory, network reach, inborn niche tolerance.
- First wave (`tr_first_wave`, markers only): an erectus-like stock spreads from East Africa over warm and temperate
  land (mean > 3 C, under 55 degrees, never into Sahul or the Americas); 2-6 seeds by farthest-point picks split it
  into species with drifted cards (cold: stocky, heavier; islands: small). They fade where sapiens comes (10% of a
  cell a 25-year step), and at the end of the dispersal any within 6 cells of sapiens is gone, dated ~1,000-3,000
  years after sapiens arrived nearby. Relics survive only in land nobody reached. arc / arc0 per cell (now / first).
- Second wave: the v0.33 dispersal (25-year steps), but split targets are scored by room for this people with its
  niche, not by a random lineage heading; peoples fill to 90% of their land (60% in the wet tropics).
- Bands (`Band`, `tr_bands_month`): ~45 people (Marlowe 2005: median 30, mean 48), at most 24 a people. Each month
  a band hill-climbs over tiles (the camp and 3 of its 6 neighbours per step; 25-120 km for foragers, 10-50 for
  collectors, by hunger, over the tile size); a camp wears out (-12% food a month there, up to 4 months), so camps
  move ~5 times a year; it remembers the best tile per season. Food: the cell's month food for this people (techs,
  niche) shared by the people in the cell, limited to the land within the band's reach (APP = 80 km2 a person, up
  to 10x in thin land). The people's range each year = cells with 2+ band-months (top 24).
- Niches (`tr_niche`, `tr_eff`, `tr_adapt_year`): per-cell demands from mean temperature, height, rain, tree cover
  and coast share. Food x the product over cold, high, dry, forest of (1 - 0.85 d (1 - e)); sea and shore food x
  (0.35 + 0.65 e_coast). e = learnt adaptation + tech cover + species tolerance. Learnt adaptation moves toward last
  year's exposure at 0.0006 a year x pace (down at a quarter of that), capped 0.45 for cold and dry, 0.7 otherwise.
- Techs (30 bits; NEEDS, MAJOR, KEEP_N, tech_cover, tr_mult, tr_want): want = the need it answers x the lineage's
  lean (0.3-1.7) toward that answer. Invention 1e-5 x want x (connected/1000)^0.27 a real year; spread along ties
  ~250 real years a hop, only where wanted; loss when too few are in touch (Henrich), past the Kline-Boyd hold
  (9 x (connected/1000)^0.27 + 2) or when no longer needed. All x the culture pace (`cult()` = pl_rate clamped 1-30:
  20 at the glacial maximum, ~16 in the melt). Pottery only for collectors.
- Tropical disease is density-dependent (holds tropical peoples to ~60% of their land's food).
- UI: Peoples map (48 km: the land wears the lineage colour, camps brighter; 16 km: camps as dots, trails softer;
  archaic land olive), P key, panel: bands and moves, "Fitted to" bars, 30 tools (majors first), species card, the
  archaic people of the tile. Events 74 classes 10 (at home in a niche), 11 (last of an archaic species), 12 (a
  meeting). Species cards reach the main thread with the lineages (state.speciesCards) for event names.
- Save block 23 version 3 (species, adaptation, exposure, visits, bands, arc, arc0); version 2 loads (bands laid out
  and adaptation learnt at the first month). ow_tribe_info is 64 floats; new ow_species_info, ow_species_n,
  ow_archaic_at.
- Cost: ~43 ms a month at 48 km (~20 of it the year end), ~60 ms at 16 km; dispersal ~3.5-4.5 s at 48 km.
- Known weaknesses (v0.34): a slow drift down after the start (-0.1 to -0.3% a year over the first 40 years);
  forest adaptation is rarely learnt (bands avoid dense forest); wells and poison darts seldom appear; hunting techs
  (spear-thrower, bow) end up in ~90% of peoples; raid deaths ~5-7% (Bowles ~14%); the Americas are reached in most
  seeds (the dispersal uses the start's coasts).

## Full fix list (updated 3 Oct 2026), most important first
People (tribes.rs)
1. (done in v0.34: spread by niche, mobile bands, techs by need.) Next: the six dials and the surplus loop.
2. Raid deaths ~5-7% of all deaths (v0.34); Bowles 2009 says ~14% on average. Raise raid rates or lethality where crowded.
3. (v0.34: disease is density-dependent; tropics ~1.3-1.5x temperate density, still a little high.)
4. The dispersal overshoots, then numbers drift down ~0.16%/yr for centuries. Start the watched world nearer
   balance (clamp the dispersal to ~0.9 of capacity, not 1.05).
5. Too many people: ~3.5-5.5 M world, Europe ~3x Tallavaara 2015's LGM estimate (~130,000).
6. The dispersal uses the start date's climate and coasts, not 70,000 years of change.
7. Whether the Americas are reached depends on the seed; check the ice-free corridor and the coastal route.
8. Cells lost in raids or merges when the receiver is full (RANGE_MAX) go to nobody (tribes.rs tr_merge, tr_raid).
9. tr.cp (people per cell) goes stale within a year when cells are dropped; only affects room estimates.
10. The year end costs ~0.25 s with ~9,000 peoples at 48 km; the dispersal ~2 s at month 1 freezes the clock.
UI
11. (done in v0.34: the rail scrolls with a visible bar; P key.)
12. Names on the map are HTML labels that can sit under the panels.
13. The panel's "Since" date is rough (bands are real since v0.34).
Nature
14. Calved icebergs drift toward the equator and melt too slowly (more.rs).
15. No lake or river water temperature and no ocean depth layers; needed for fish, whales, corals, sea ice,
    storms, and later for people (fishing seasons, shipping).
16. Animals are pools, not species; seals do not migrate or haul out; little animal disease.
17. A culled herd region refills in about a year by migration (fine for a cull that leaves the grass, but check).
18. Whale numbers are propped up by density dependence (sea.rs wh_dm); the krill under the ice may still be low.
19. The forager food model is ~4x recorded densities everywhere (forager_check by band); people use 0.1 of it.
Engine
20. Loads drift from a straight run after a few years (half/byte-precision tile state); tools/state_diff.js lists
    the fields; a fix roughly doubles the save.
21. bio_check speed fails: ~96 ms a month at 48 km against a 90 ms budget (same since v0.31; world without people).
22. Older open items in TODO.md (Nile ~30% high, Patagonia too warm at the LGM, Sichuan fog, rarity ladder
    rerun, cyclone landfalls high, 16 km stalls when coasts move).
